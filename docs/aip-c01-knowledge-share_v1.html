Introduction

What this certification is about

At its core: integrating foundation models into real applications and business workflows — putting GenAI into production on AWS.

  • The role it targets: a GenAI developer who puts FMs to work in real systems — not someone training models from scratch.
  • The emphasis is practical — implementing GenAI solutions into production environments using AWS technologies.
  • The underlying stance: the FM is one component inside a larger system — not the whole system.

What it validates

Eight capabilities you're expected to have

1Design & build with vector stores, RAG, knowledge bases, and other GenAI architectures
2Integrate FMs into applications and business workflows
3Prompt engineering & prompt management
4Implement agentic AI solutions
5Optimize for cost, performance, and business value
6Security, governance & Responsible AI
7Troubleshoot, monitor & optimize GenAI applications
8Evaluate FMs for quality and responsibility

A useful checklist for what our team needs if we take GenAI seriously.
